Privacy Policy & Text Messaging Terms

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Wildrock LLC (“Wildrock,” “we”) collects, uses and protects information when you work with us, email us, call us or text us. Wildrock designs and builds homes in Northwest Arkansas.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, company, phone number, email address and business address.
  • Project communications, including emails, text messages, photos and documents you send us (for example bids, contracts, invoices and lien waivers).
  • Call recordings and transcripts. Calls to or from our business number may be recorded and transcribed so we have an accurate record of project details.
  • Business records such as license, insurance and payment information needed to hire and pay for work.

How we use it

We use this information only to run our construction projects: requesting and reviewing bids, scheduling work, coordinating site access and inspections, sharing plans, managing contracts, and processing invoices and payments. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us operate (for example our phone, messaging and email providers), with project parties when needed to complete the work (such as our lender, inspectors or design professionals), or when we believe disclosure is required by law or needed to protect our rights. We share information with service providers for the purpose of providing services to us.

Text messaging (SMS) terms

Program: Wildrock sends and receives text messages with subcontractors, suppliers and project partners about active or prospective projects, such as scheduling, site updates, bid requests, plan sharing, invoices and payments. We do not send marketing messages.

How you opt in: You agree to receive texts from Wildrock when you give us your mobile number for project communication (for example on a bid, quote, business card, contract or in conversation) or when you text our business number first.

Message frequency varies with project activity. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out, reply STOP at any time. You will receive a confirmation, and texts from that number will stop unless you opt back in by replying START. Opting out of texts does not affect communications by phone, email or mail about work in progress. For help, reply HELP or email us at the address below.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared with subcontractors that support our services, such as our messaging provider, only to deliver messages.

Call recording

Calls with Wildrock’s business number may be recorded and transcribed for accuracy and record keeping. By continuing a call with our business number, you consent to it being recorded. If you prefer not to be recorded, please contact us by email instead.

Retention and security

We keep project communications and records for as long as we consider necessary for the project, warranty periods, and legal, tax, insurance or lending purposes. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information sent to or stored by us or our service providers.

Please do not send bank account numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive financial information by text message or email. Always confirm any request to change payment instructions by calling a phone number you already know is ours.

Your choices

You may ask us to update your contact information or stop texting you. You may also ask us to delete your information; we will consider reasonable requests, subject to records we are required or permitted to keep for legal, contractual, financial or project purposes.

Important limitations

  • Contracts control. Text messages, emails and calls are for convenience and coordination. They do not amend, waive or replace any written contract, change order or purchase order, and they do not satisfy any formal notice requirement unless the applicable contract expressly says so.
  • No guarantee of delivery. Text messages, calls and emails depend on carriers and third-party providers and may be delayed, filtered or not delivered. Do not rely on a text message for anything time-critical or safety-related; call directly.
  • Third-party services. We are not responsible for the practices of carriers, messaging, phone or email providers, or other third parties, which are governed by their own terms and policies.
  • Limitation of liability. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Wildrock is not liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential damages arising from our text messaging program, call recording, or the handling of information described in this policy.
  • Our communications are intended for businesses and adults, not for children under 13.
  • This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Arkansas. It is a notice of our practices, not a contract, and does not create rights for any third party.
