# Direction F: WILD/ROCK Stacked Contrast. Palette

The mark is drawn in one colour first. The palette is built like the logo: an open, light layer (sky and sand) over a dense, grounded layer (granite and basalt). One small, warm signal colour is used sparingly.

| # | Name | Hex | Role | Rationale |
|---|------|-----|------|-----------|
| 1 | **Basalt** | `#232624` | Primary ink. The logo, body text, yard-sign panels, dark backgrounds | A near-black charcoal with a slight green-grey cast, like wet stone. It is softer and more natural than pure black, but just as authoritative. It is the "ROCK" layer. |
| 2 | **Granite** | `#6E716D` | Secondary text, rules, drafting linework, captions | A mid grey taken from weathered granite. It carries information without shouting, the quiet voice of a studio. |
| 3 | **Mesa Sand** | `#E7DDCB` | Primary light ground. Paper, sign faces, the logo on dark | A high-desert sand. It is warm and human, and it keeps the system away from cold, corporate white. |
| 4 | **High Sky** | `#9FB0B8` | Landscape accent. Hero fields, the optional "WILD" tone in two-tone use on dark, illustration | A dusty blue-grey, like distant ridgelines in haze. It is the "WILD" layer: open air and distance. Use it on Basalt, never as text on Sand (too little contrast). |
| 5 | **Ember** | `#C8572A` | Signal only. "Now building" tags, a single rule, active states, at most about 5% of any surface | A warm high-desert orange, like late sun on rock. It is human warmth and a precise point of attention, so it is used rarely to stay meaningful. It is never used for the logo itself. |

## Usage rules
- **One colour first:** the logo is Basalt on Sand/white, or Sand/white on Basalt. The one-colour white file is `mono-white.svg`.
- **Two-tone (optional, dark grounds only):** WILD in High Sky and ROCK in Mesa Sand. This repeats the strata idea in colour. It is never used below 120px wide.
- **Proportion:** about 60% Sand, 25% Basalt, 10% Granite/Sky, and at most 5% Ember.
- **Contrast:** Basalt on Sand is 11.4:1. Granite on Sand is 3.7:1, so use it for large text, rules and UI only. High Sky on Basalt is 6.8:1. Ember on Sand is 3.2:1, so use it for marks and large type, never body copy.
