# Direction B: Faceted W (palette and type)

## Palette

Forest and slate do the work. Clay is the one warm accent. The mark is always one flat colour, Deep Forest on light and Bone on dark.

| # | Name | Hex | Role |
|---|------|-----|------|
| 1 | Deep Forest | `#1E2A25` | Primary. The mark, wordmark, headlines and dark grounds (yard sign, avatar, footer). |
| 2 | Basalt Slate | `#3F4B4E` | Secondary. Body text, UI chrome and secondary dark surfaces. |
| 3 | Fired Clay | `#B0694A` | Accent, used sparingly: one rule, one button or one detail per layout. It is never used for the mark. |
| 4 | Lichen | `#A8B2A1` | A soft supporting tint for dividers, landscape layers, illustration and quiet fills. |
| 5 | Bone | `#F3F0E8` | Paper and ground. Page backgrounds, and the reversed mark on Forest. |

Approximate proportions: Bone 60%, Forest 25%, Slate 8%, Lichen 5%, Clay 2%.
Contrast: Forest on Bone is about 13:1 and Slate on Bone is about 7.9:1, so both pass AA for body text. Clay on Bone is about 3.7:1, so use it for large text or UI accents only, not body copy.

## Type pairing

- **Inter Tight SemiBold (600), all caps, widely tracked:** the WILDROCK wordmark, navigation, labels and signage. It is drafting-table precise: narrow, even and architectural.
- **Fraunces Light (300) and Light Italic:** editorial headlines and the line *Built around how you live.* Its soft, slightly old-style serif brings the human, conversational voice.
- Body copy: Inter Tight Regular (400) at 15–17px with generous leading.

## Rationale

- **Precise.** The mark is built from three flat planes at one shared angle and separated by equal cuts. It reads like a stone that has been measured and cleaved, not carved by hand. The tracked sans wordmark keeps the studio rigour.
- **Wild.** The central plane is a summit. The chiselled tops of the outer blades rise to two inner high points, so the silhouette reads as a ridgeline: peak, saddle, peak. Forest, slate and lichen come from the ridge and the tree line rather than from the quarry.
- **Human.** The palette is warm, not corporate. Bone replaces white, and a single clay accent feels hand-fired. Fraunces italic carries the client-facing voice, so the brand listens before it specifies.
