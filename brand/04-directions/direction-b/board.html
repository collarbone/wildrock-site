Wildrock · Logo direction B of 3

Faceted W

A monogram cut like stone. Three flat planes share one angle and are separated by clean negative-space cuts. Two chiselled outer blades flank a central peak, so the letter becomes a ridgeline: peak, saddle, summit, saddle, peak. It sits with a quiet, widely tracked WILDROCK wordmark in a mark-and-word pair.

Precise

The three planes share one angle and equal cuts. It reads as cleaved and measured, the way a drafting studio works. There are no hairlines and no ornament.

Wild

The central plane is a summit, and the chisel cuts lift the outer blades into twin high points. The landscape is there as a hint, not a picture.

Human

Warm bone and a single clay accent replace corporate white and blue. A soft serif carries the conversational voice: Built around how you live.
