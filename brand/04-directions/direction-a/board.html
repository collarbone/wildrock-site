Direction A

Strata

Four bold bands of rock, cut cleanly by two canyons. In the space they leave, a W appears. The mark is landform built on a drafting grid: wild ground, drawn with a studio’s precision.

HumanThe W is found, not stamped on. It shows up when you look, the way a good home shows the life lived in it.
WildSediment layers, canyon cuts, a horizon base. It reads as place, not as a picture of a mountain.
PreciseOne module throughout: 11-unit bands, 4-unit joints, and canyon walls on a single shared angle.
01 · Construction

Built on a 64-unit grid

Four 11-unit strata with 4-unit joints sit on a 64 × 64 square. Two V-cuts, both on one angle, run through the top three bands. The base band stays whole, as the horizon and the foundation. Where the cuts meet in the second band they leave a flat-topped middle summit, so the gap above reads as a W. Every edge is horizontal or follows that one angle, and there are no hairlines, so it holds up in vinyl, in stone and at favicon size.

03 · Lockups

Mark and word, side by side

In the horizontal lockup, the wordmark’s cap height matches the two middle strata exactly (units 19–45), so the word sits on the mark’s own grid. The gap between mark and word is two band widths. Clear space on every side is one full band plus one joint.

04 · Palette

Stone, shade and one living green

The palette is mostly mineral, with neutrals taken from limestone, travertine and basalt. Juniper is the one landscape accent. It is the colour of high-desert scrub and forest edges, and it is used sparingly.

Basalt#23221F

The primary colour for the mark, type and signage. A warm charcoal, never pure black.

Limestone#ECE8E1

Main ground for paper, the web and reversed marks.

Travertine#CDBFA9

A warm neutral for surfaces, materials and print stock.

Slate#77736B

Secondary text, rules and drawing linework.

Juniper#4B5B48

The accent. Use it on 10% of surfaces or less.

05 · Typography

Outfit & Newsreader

Outfit (Google Fonts) is for the wordmark and UI. Its pure circular O and C and its straight, even strokes carry the mark’s geometry. Its open apertures keep it warm, not cold or corporate. Set in caps with wide tracking, it reads as calm and exclusive. Newsreader is for editorial and body text. It is a literary serif and gives the studio lead’s voice: human, considered, timeless.
