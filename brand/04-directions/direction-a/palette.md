# Direction A: Strata — palette & type

## Colour

The palette is mostly mineral with one living accent. Get the form right in **Basalt on Limestone** first. Colour is a second layer.

| # | Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Basalt** | `#23221F` | Primary colour for the mark, wordmark, body text, yard-sign panels and dark grounds. A warm charcoal, never pure black. |
| 2 | **Limestone** | `#ECE8E1` | Main light ground (web, paper, sign lettering) and the colour of the reversed mark. |
| 3 | **Travertine** | `#CDBFA9` | Warm neutral for secondary surfaces, print stock, material and interior backdrops, and the tagline on dark. |
| 4 | **Slate** | `#77736B` | Secondary text, captions, rules and drafting linework. |
| 5 | **Juniper** | `#4B5B48` | The one landscape accent, from high-desert scrub and forest edges. Use it on 10% of surfaces or less: a CTA, a single page section, vehicle or site hoarding. |

Approximate proportions: Limestone 50% · Basalt 30% · Travertine 10% · Slate 5% · Juniper 5%.
The mark must only appear as Basalt, Limestone or white (`#FFFFFF`, on photography). Never use Juniper on Basalt for the mark, because the contrast is too low.

## Type pairing

- **Outfit** (Google Fonts, 500/400/300) is for the wordmark reference, UI, navigation, labels and signage. Set caps with wide tracking (+0.2 to +0.34em).
  - *Why:* its circular O and C, straight even strokes and flat-cut joins match the mark’s geometry (Precise). Its open apertures and soft overall colour keep it from feeling like a bank or tech brand. Wide tracking in caps gives the calm, exclusive, *quiet over bold* tone from the workshop.
- **Newsreader** (Google Fonts, 400 + italic) is for headlines, editorial and body text, and the tagline *Built around how you live.*
  - *Why:* a literary serif carries the voice of a design-studio lead who listens first (Human). It is timeless rather than current.

The logo wordmark is a **drawn master** made of geometric paths (rectangles, parallelograms and circular arcs on a 40-unit cap height with a 5.6-unit stem). It follows Outfit’s proportions, so the logo never depends on a font being installed.

## Rationale: Human · Wild · Precise

- **Wild**: four strata cut by two canyons. The mark reads as ground, sediment and horizon. It is a sense of place without drawing a mountain or a house.
- **Precise**: one module throughout. Bands are 11 units, joints are 4 units, and every canyon wall runs on one shared angle on a 64-unit grid. There are no hairlines, so it survives vinyl, stone engraving and a 16px favicon.
- **Human**: the W isn’t stamped on. It shows in the negative space when you look, the way a well-made home shows the life lived in it. The calm horizontal rhythm feels steady and timeless, not loud.
