Horizon Wordmark

A custom geometric wordmark, wide and evenly weighted like a modern architecture studio's. It has one ownable detail: the O in ROCK is a solid disc, part stone and part sun, cut by a single horizon line. The same horizon sets the height of the R's bowl and the K's junction, so the whole word rests on one line. The companion mark turns the detail inside out: the disc is cut out of a square, like a landscape framed by a window.

Human

Open counters, generous spacing and a calm rhythm. The disc reads as a sun over a place someone has chosen to live, a personal view rather than a corporate symbol.

Wild

The landscape lives in the detail: one horizon line through a sun or stone. It points to ridgelines and high desert without drawing them, and without any rustic clichés.

Precise

Built on a 100-unit cap height with 18-unit strokes. The horizon sits at 60 units across the O, the R and the K. In the lockup the mark's disc matches the O exactly, so the two horizons line up.

01 · Wordmark

WILDROCK, drawn as custom letterforms

Every letter is a hand-built SVG path: rectangles, polygons and arcs only, with no font. The O is the only filled form in the word, so it becomes the stone.

02 · Companion mark

The disc on the horizon, framed

The mark inverts the O: in the wordmark the disc is solid and the horizon is cut out of it, while in the mark the disc is cut out and the horizon stays solid. The 16 px favicon is a separate, tuned drawing with a larger disc and a 2 px horizon.

03 · Lockups

Mark-word pair, on light and on dark

Horizontal lockup: the tile is sized so its disc is exactly the size of the O, which puts both horizons on one continuous line. Minimum clear space is half the cap height on every side.

04 · Palette

Limestone and ink, with the land as accent

Ink#1C1B19The primary colour, for the mark, type and signage panels. It is warm black, never pure #000.
Limestone#EEE9E0The ground, for paper, web backgrounds and reversed marks.
Desert Clay#A65A3AThe single accent, used sparingly: the sun, a call to action, one detail per layout.
Sage#7E8B74A landscape secondary, for environmental graphics, illustration and site hoarding.
Sandstone#CDBFA8A supporting neutral, for rules, cards, tints and material swatches.

Rough usage ratio: Limestone 52 · Ink 28 · Sandstone 10 · Sage 6 · Clay 4. The logo is always one colour, either Ink or Limestone. Clay is reserved for the scenery around the logo.
