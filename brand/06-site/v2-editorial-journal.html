03 How it works

The process, in four chapters.

A clear path from first idea to move-in day, so you feel calm and in control at every step.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Consult

    We meet on site, listen to how you want to live, and talk candidly about scope, timing and budget range.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Design & Budget

    Plans, selections and a detailed fixed-price proposal, so you know exactly what you’re getting before we break ground.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Build

    Our crews and trusted trades get to work, with weekly updates, a clean site and one point of contact throughout.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Walkthrough & Warranty

    A detailed final walkthrough, a punch list finished to your satisfaction, and a written warranty behind our work.
