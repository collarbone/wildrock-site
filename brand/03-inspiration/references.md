## Takeaways for the directions step
- **Form:** solid, heavy marks that read at 16px. Faceted or strata geometry beats illustration (Sequoia, Arc'teryx, Mork-Ulnes).
- **Wordmark:** plain caps with generous tracking, or a wide grotesk, with one ownable cut or notch (Kvadrat, Vitra, Archivo-expanded).
- **Colour:** start one-colour charcoal on stone. A single landscape accent (sage or clay) at most.
- **Imagery:** horizon, strata and timber, black and white or muted. Never a finished house as the hero.
