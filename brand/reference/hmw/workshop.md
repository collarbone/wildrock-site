## 1. Target: who do we want to reach?
- Design managers
- In-house product designers
  - Collaborative
  - Has something to prove
  - Sees themselves as an expert

## 2. Issue
The core problem the brand can solve for the target, and the key consumer insights it serves.
- Creating a plan around creative tasks is challenging
- Standard documents and wiki pages lack workflow-specific optimizations
- Estimating timelines is very challenging
- Communicating where you are and what is left to do is tough
- Staying organized while working on multiple projects is tough

## 3. Solution
The brand idea that answers the top-of-mind value prop in a clear, compelling statement.

> **Focus on the work, not the workflow.**

## 4. Personality
If the solution were a physical person, what traits would they have? What would they look and sound like?

### Notes
- Person archetype: Positive · Smart · Attentive · Present · Helpful
- "Butler": Supportive · Responsive · Dynamic
- Brainstorm: Vibrant · Analytical · Experimental · High-end · Niche · Specialized · Geeky · Calm · Scholarly · Focused · Effortless · Playful

**How does the target currently feel, how do they want to feel, and what imagery fits?**

| Currently feel | Want to feel | Imagery |
|---|---|---|
| Underwater | They can breathe | Space, nature, scenic |
| Overwhelmed | Got a handle on it: in control, calm, at ease | |
| Stuck | Making solid progress (consistent, forward motion) | Stairs, cog, blade |
| Letting others down | Magnifying their calling, going above and beyond | Flying, going up, expanding / growing |
| Behind | Actually ahead and able to do more (wealth mindset, abundance, sharing) | |

Imagery clusters:
- **Space:** canyons, trees, roots, mountains
- **Calm:** zen garden, water, clouds. It doesn't excite; it reassures and stays constant.

### Final traits
1. **Calm**: Abundance · In control · Secure
2. **Experimental**: Detailed · Analytical · Geeky
3. **Vibrant**: Free · Flexible · Adaptable
