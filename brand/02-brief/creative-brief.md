# Wildrock: Brand Identity Creative Brief

> Follows the template in [../reference/hmw/creative-brief.md](../reference/hmw/creative-brief.md). Built from [../01-workshop/workshop.md](../01-workshop/workshop.md).

## A. Basic info
1. **Company name:** Wildrock. The descriptor can change ("Builders", "Homes", "Custom Homes"), so the mark must stand on "Wildrock" alone.
2. **What we do:** custom residential homes for move-up and luxury clients, designed and built around how each client lives.
3. **Mission:** Design and build homes around the people who live in them.
4. **Who we target:** move-up / luxury buyers and custom-home clients.
5. **Brand idea:** *Built around how you live.*

## B. Brand personality
1. **Human**: life-first · understanding · personal
2. **Wild**: landscape · freedom · sense of place
3. **Precise**: craft · detail · modern studio

Tone: timeless, quiet, exclusive, steady. The voice is a modern design studio lead.

## C. General direction: what type of logo?
Explore these (numbers match the [logo-type chart](../reference/hmw/images/)):
- **1/2. Monogram or wordmark:** a W / WR monogram, or a custom WILDROCK wordmark
- **4. Abstract mark:** a geometric symbol suggesting ridge, strata or landform
- **8. Mark-word pair:** symbol plus wordmark lockup (in the spirit of Sequoia and Ironclad)

## D. Do not try 🚫
- Literal house or roof icons (no roofline-and-chimney clip art)
- Rustic or Western clichés (antlers, barnwood, cowboy type)
- Styles that are too thin or intricate. The mark must survive a yard sign at a distance and a 16px favicon.
- Cold corporate looks (bank, tech startup)

## E. Imagery ideas
- Wild landscape: ridgelines, canyons, strata, horizon lines
- Craft and materials: timber, stone, drafting linework, precise joinery
- Simple, confident geometry; calm and timeless

## F. Placement
- **Jobsite yard sign:** the primary physical touchpoint, read from the street
- **Website, social avatar, favicon:** includes tiny sizes, so the mark must work as a standalone square

## G. Logomark concept ideas
- An abstract mark where landform (ridge or strata) meets precise geometry
- A W monogram built from ridge or facet lines
- A custom wordmark with one subtle, ownable detail (for example a cut, notch or ligature that suggests rock or ridge)
- Letters set in, on, or next to a simple shape

**Colours:** nail the form in one colour first, then explore. Expect a restrained, high-end palette: stone, charcoal, warm neutrals, with possibly one landscape accent.

## H. What success looks like
- An **ownable symbol**: the mark alone is recognisable on a sign, an avatar or a favicon
- **Subtle nods to "wild" and "rock"**, never literal
- **Reads as a high-end studio** that could sit beside top architecture firms' marks
- **Works in one colour** (watermark, vinyl, no gradients)
- Reflects the traits: Human · Wild · Precise
