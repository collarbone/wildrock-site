# Wildrock: Brand Personality Workshop

> Follows the template in [../reference/hmw/workshop.md](../reference/hmw/workshop.md). Answers come from the owner interview on 2026-11-17.

## 1. Target: who do we want to reach?
- **Move-up / luxury buyers**: successful professionals upgrading to a high-end home with real design ambitions
- **Custom-home clients**: people commissioning a one-of-a-kind home rather than buying spec

What they're like:
- They know how they want to live, and they're frustrated when houses don't reflect it
- They value design, craftsmanship and a calm, professional process
- They expect a premium, studio-like experience

## 2. Issue
The core problem Wildrock solves:
- **Homes aren't designed for the people who live in them.** Most houses are built around floor-plan templates and resale averages, not around the activities their owners value or how they want to live.
- Clients end up with compromises: rooms they never use, and no space for what matters to them.
- The building process feels generic and anxious instead of personal and controlled.

## 3. Solution
The opposite of the issue: human-centred design, so the home is shaped around your life.

> **Built around how you live.**

## 4. Personality

### If Wildrock were a person
**A modern design studio lead.** Sharp, editorial, with curated taste. High-end and minimal. They listen first, then design with precision.

### How clients feel, and how they want to feel
| Currently feel | Want to feel |
|---|---|
| Compromised | **Understood** |
| Generic | **Uniquely theirs** |
| Anxious | **Calm & in control** |

### Imagery
- **Wild landscape:** ridgelines, canyons, high desert, forest edges. This is the "wild".
- **Craft & materials:** timber joinery, stone, blueprints and linework, precise drafting. This is the "rock" and the "precise".

### Final traits
1. **Human**: life-first · understanding · personal
2. **Wild**: landscape · freedom · sense of place
3. **Precise**: craft · detail · modern studio
