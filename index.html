How it works

Our Process

A clear, four-step path from idea to move-in day.

  1. 01

    Consult

    We meet on site, listen to your goals, and talk candidly about scope, timing, and budget range.

  2. 02

    Design & Budget

    Plans, selections, and a detailed fixed-price proposal, so you know exactly what you're getting before we break ground.

  3. 03

    Build

    Our crews and trusted trades get to work, with weekly updates, a clean jobsite, and one point of contact throughout.

  4. 04

    Walkthrough & Warranty

    A detailed final walkthrough, a punch list completed to your satisfaction, and a written warranty backing our work.
